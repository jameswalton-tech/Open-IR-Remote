# Data and specification licensing

The Open IR Remote format specification, schema and original contributed signal records are offered under CC0 1.0 unless a file or provenance record states otherwise.

Repository software remains GPL-3.0 under the root `LICENSE` file.

Third-party data is not automatically covered by CC0. Every imported record must preserve its source, source revision and licence. A source link is not permission to redistribute its contents.

Remote images require explicit provenance and a stated rights basis. Accepted bases are an open licence, direct permission, public domain, or an identification-only basis reviewed by maintainers. Do not submit an image merely because it is publicly visible online. Copyright remains with the respective rights holder unless the image record expressly says otherwise.
